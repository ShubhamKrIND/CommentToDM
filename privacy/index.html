Privacy Policy

CommentToDM ("we", "us", or "our") is a product of Sbl.so. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our platform.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password when you register.
  • LinkedIn account credentials — OAuth tokens required to connect your LinkedIn account to our platform. We store access tokens but never your LinkedIn password.
  • DM content — the message templates you create within CommentToDM to send to commenters.
  • Billing information — payment details processed securely through our payment provider (Stripe). We do not store card numbers.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, actions taken within the platform.
  • Log data — IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and timestamps.
  • Cookies — session cookies for authentication and analytics cookies (see Section 6).

LinkedIn data accessed via API

When you connect your LinkedIn account, we access the following with your explicit authorization:

  • Your LinkedIn profile (name, profile URL)
  • Comments on your LinkedIn posts
  • Ability to send direct messages on your behalf to users who commented on your posts

We access commenters' first names solely to personalize the DM you've pre-written. We do not store commenter profile data beyond what is needed to deliver the message.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the CommentToDM service
  • To authenticate your account and maintain your session
  • To detect and monitor comments matching your configured triggers
  • To send DMs on your behalf to commenters who triggered your keywords
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To send transactional emails (receipts, account alerts)
  • To improve and optimize the platform
  • To respond to support requests
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data to train AI models.

3. Data sharing

We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers — we use Stripe (payments), AWS (infrastructure), and analytics providers. Each is bound by data processing agreements.
  • LinkedIn — we communicate with LinkedIn's API on your behalf as authorized by you.
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.
  • Business transfer — in the event of a merger or acquisition, user data may transfer to the new entity.

4. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., billing records for up to 7 years).

DM send logs are retained for 90 days for debugging and support purposes, then automatically deleted.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for LinkedIn tokens at rest
  • Access controls limiting employee access to user data
  • Regular security reviews

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to protecting your data.

6. Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary — session and authentication cookies required for the service to function.
  • Analytics — anonymized usage data to understand how users interact with the platform. You can opt out via your browser settings.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to certain processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@commenttodm.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's privacy

CommentToDM is not directed at children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware that a child under 16 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

9. International transfers

CommentToDM is operated from India. If you are accessing the service from the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or other regions with data protection laws, your information may be transferred to and processed in India. By using the service, you consent to this transfer.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or a prominent notice in the app. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
